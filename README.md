## MODULE 1 & 2: CMS and NGO & Feature Needed for CMS and NGO website
* 1.1 CMS (Content management System): A software that allows users to create, manage, and modify the digital content of a platform without needing specialized technical knowledge.
* 1.2 NGO: While developing a CMS website for an NGO, the learner must clearly understand the mission, target audience, technical requirement, and how the CMS will support goals.
## Purpose of NGO
 * Education:  Opening schools and offering training for children and adults.
      Vision: A world where every single child has equal access to high-quality learning opportunities.
      Mission: to build school, train local teachers, and distribute learning materials to remote villages.
 * Healthcare:  High infant mortality, lack of clean water, and preventable disease outbreaks.
      Vision: A healthy world free from preventable illnesses where medical care is a universal right.
      Mission: To establish mobile health clinics, deliver vaccines, and train community healthcare workers.
 *Environment:  Accelerated climate change, species extinction, and plastic pollution in oceans.
      Vision: A sustainable planet where human societies live in perfect harmony with natural ecosystems.
      Mission: To restore degraded forests, lobby for green policies, and run plastic cleanup campaigns.

** Key Objective: Raise Awareness, Mobilize Resource, Attract Volunteers, Engage Stakeholders.
** Target Audience: Donor, Volunteer, Beneficiaries, and partner Organization. 
## Key Feature Needed 
 * Content Management: Easy creation, editing, and publishing of content by non-technical staff and support multimedia                            content (images, videos and document.
 * Donation Management: Secure payment forms for one-time or monthly donations with automatic digital receipts
 * Volunteer and Events Managements: Forms to captures volunteer interest and events registrations and a calendar to show                                       upcoming charity events.
 * Impact Tracking and Transparency: Clear dashboard to display statistic, success, stories, reports and downloadable                                           reports (e.g., PDF for impact summaries).
 * Search Engine Organization (SEO): A tool to ensure the site rank well on search engine.
